A wise observation for this Sunday..

Sunday, September 17, 200610:08 AM PDT

Any time that OPEC got a little too overzealous in pushing up oil prices back in the 1970’s, the legendary Saudi oil minister Sheik Ahmed Zaki Yamani was fond of telling his colleagues: Remember, the Stone Age didn’t end because we ran out of stones. What he meant was that the Stone Age ended because people invented alternative tools. The oil age is also not going to end because we run out of oil. It will end because the price of oil goes so high that people invent alternatives.

— Thomas Friedman, “The Energy Harvest”

Well said. Very well said.

- Anand
