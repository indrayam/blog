What happened to Anand’s Blogging?

Monday, September 10, 200710:42 PM PDT

I am alive and well, and as much as I would like pick on an easy target for procrastinations in my blogging habits, it’s really not her fault at all. She is been super nice and kind to the two of us ever since she arrived. It’s really Cisco’s fault. Cisco launched an internal company blogging platform earlier this month, and in the last couple of weeks, I have blogged almost 50 times!! I guess I could double-post some of my entries here as well, but I just have not been able to do that. In any case, just thought I would set the record straight.
