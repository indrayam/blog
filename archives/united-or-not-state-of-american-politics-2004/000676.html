What about Colorado?

Wednesday, October 20, 200410:55 AM PDT


9news.com: Still more registered Republicans
in Colorado than Democrats

Can Senator Kerry win Colorado? You decide.

President Bush had won this State by almost 145,000 votes in 2000. Ralph Nader had 91,000 odd votes in 2000. Unfortunately for Senator Kerry, Ralph Nader is on the Colorado Ballot this year as well.

My take: Very, very unlikely. In this particular case, I would disagree with Zogby. Senator Kerry would be better leaving this state off his itinerary between now and the Election Day.

- Anand
