Theme of the day @ RNC: Land of Opportunity..;-)

Wednesday, September 1, 200410:58 PM PDT

[via Optimism]



Take it easy guys. It’s just an Election after all

I think I get it. The message from RNC this week which came out loud-and-clear night after night is this:

Forget about Kerry’s Vietnam war services, his intellectual capacity. Trust us. We know better. The guy is a “confused frost-bite liberal bum” who can’t make up his mind on anything. Look at him. The guy wakes up every morning only to go back to sleep at night. Why did he wake up in the first place? What a flip-flopper.

Forget about Bush’s Vietnam war no-services, his intellectual incapacity. Trust us. We know better. The guy is a bonafide (mis)leader. When he says something, he sticks with it. Never mind the dubyaspeak. Never mind the consequences.

From a Republican standpoint, you could not have made them prouder. Ofcourse, there is still this little thing that needs to be done - Win the election! All the best. Trust me, you will need it.

Note:

Mr. Miller, you don’t have much to worry. Either way, you will emerge victorious! So, relax.

- Anand
