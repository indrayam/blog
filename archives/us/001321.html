Wonder why these guys don’t speak out when they’re within the Administration

Sunday, June 10, 20071:59 AM PDT

Guantanamo has become a major, major problem … in the way the world perceives America and if it were up to me I would close Guantanamo not tomorrow but this afternoon … and I would not let any of those people go. I would simply move them to the United States and put them into our federal legal system. Essentially, we have shaken the belief the world had in America’s justice system by keeping a place like Guantanamo open and creating things like the military commission. We don’t need it and it is causing us far more damage than any good we get for it.

— Colin Powell, “Colin Powell says Guantanamo should be closed”

Duh…

- Anand
