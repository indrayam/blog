Confession

Saturday, September 18, 200410:52 AM PDT

I don’t know about you, but the more I read news or polls or hear folks praise President Bush’s leadership in Iraq and Senator Kerry’s lack of “a” position on Iraq, it makes my head hurt and makes me want to scream at the top of my lungs.

What’s the matter with America(ns)?

War on Terror, Economy, Iraq, Environment, Education and Health Care - do you really think that America is better off today in each of these categories than it was four years ago?

Ugh..
