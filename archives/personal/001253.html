Tag Cloud

Tuesday, January 16, 20075:09 PM PST

If you’re interested in knowing what I am reading, you can peruse my Linkblog. However, if you’d rather see a tag cloud view of what I am reading, you can check out the new Tag Cloud page. Why this new view? Well, let’s just say it makes it easy for folks to see what I am really reading.

While I may not blog daily, I do use delicious quite frequently. To give you an idea, I started using deli.cio.us almost two years ago and in this time I have added almost 7000 links. During the same time, I probably have added less than a 1000 blog entries. Kinda gives you an idea of how religiously I do linkblogging, than actual blogging.
