Sunday, June 27, 200412:20 PM PDT

[via Catching a wave out of pricey California]

…

Home prices have so far outstripped income growth in California that the
average worker would need to save every penny he earned for more than
eight years to buy the average house. In Wisconsin, that worker would
need less than 2 1/2 years of income to pay cash for a house.

“It’s just amazing,” said Robert Shiller, the Yale University economist who made
the calculations for a recent study. “Californians must be so stressed out.”
