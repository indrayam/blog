China helping India?

Monday, June 27, 20053:44 PM PDT

Investment is increasing into India. Politically, as China grows more, we (Japan) look to India more. We (Japan) were once Asia’s growth engine. Now it is China. One day it will be India.

— Yoriko Kawaguchi, Japanese prime minister’s assistant for foreign affairs
