Sorry..:-(

Sunday, July 10, 200510:32 PM PDT

And no, the picture above is not something that I can claim as mine. Here’s the source.

What has this picture got to do with the title of this blog entry, you ask? Well, usually I am pretty good about keeping my blog up-to-date. However, this blog saw no posts almost all of last week. So, in case you’ve been wondering, here’s the reason: I have been on a road trip for most of last week. As much as I would like to give you more details about my trip, including cool pictures that I took (and I did take a boat load of them), the fact is that I can barely type, let alone think clearly. Suffice it to say that after 5 days and 2000-odd miles long road trip that took me to places like Santa Barbara (CA), Phoenix/Sedona/Grand Canyon (AZ) and Hoover Dam/Las Vegas (NV), I am just glad to be back home.
