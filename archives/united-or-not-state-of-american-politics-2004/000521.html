Now reading…

Tuesday, September 14, 200411:45 PM PDT

I have read a good portion of this book, most of it on the plane during my trip this past weekend to Columbus, OH.

Here’s what I think: “Whatever you might think of Pat, when it comes to War in Iraq this guy gets it!” I plan on writing entries in the coming days and weeks essentially highlighting my favorite excerpts from this book.

I would highly recommend this book to anyone who would like to understand why the War in Iraq is NOT the same as War on Terror and why it was such a huge mistake to begin with.
