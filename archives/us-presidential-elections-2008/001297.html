Ugh

Friday, March 2, 200711:43 PM PST

I was going to have a few comments on the other Democratic presidential candidate John Edwards, but it turns out you have to go into rehab if you use the word ‘faggot,’ so I — so kind of an impasse, can’t really talk about Edwards.

— Ann Coulter at the annual Conservative Political Action Conference CPAC) in Washington, D.C.

I am not sure what’s the educational background of Ms. Coulter, but to have someone like her be at something that sure seems to be a pretty major congregation of conservative thinkers of this country is sad at best and dangerous at worst. I used to think that the illiterate buffoonery of a few politicians back in India was pathetic. Well, the guys here might be educated and look a whole lot more professional, but when all is said and done, their deeds in the name of patriotism, or worst, public service, is just about as bad as it gets.

- Anand
