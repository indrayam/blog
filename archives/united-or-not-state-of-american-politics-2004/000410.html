I wanted to stay out of it, but…

Tuesday, August 3, 20041:01 PM PDT

… I think it’s important that I use this forum to share my $0.02 cents on US politics (especially, the upcoming election) from time to time. This is not just any Presidential election of United States. It’s much, much more than that.

If you’re an American, I urge you to make plans to go out and vote! For god sake, don’t just throw it away. Not this time. I don’t care if you vote Republican or Democrat. As long as you do your homework and vote with your conscience, you will be doing yourself, your country and this world a great service.

If I could vote, I think you know who I would vote for
