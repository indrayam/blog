Please vote

Monday, November 6, 20067:22 PM PST

If you’re an American and can vote, here’s a little plea: Please do. And if I may, remember that while the folks that you vote for tomorrow might be going to the United States Senate or the House, they really have a much larger impact around the world, not just on US lives. If you don’t believe me, just look at Iraq. The person you elect tomorrow WILL absolutely have an effect on the lives of not only Americans, but a whole lot of people around the world. Think about it.

Thank you.

- Anand
