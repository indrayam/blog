I came here to scream..;-)

Monday, May 15, 20063:56 PM PDT

The difference between the communist and capitalist systems is that, although both give you a kick in the ass, in the communist system you have to applaud, while in the capitalist system you can scream. And I came here to scream.

— “Thank you, my foolish friends in the West”, Cuban novelist Reinaldo Arenas after managing to escape to US after years of persecution by the Cuban government for being openly homosexual and a dissident

Certainly an interesting way to look at it.

- Anand
