TeleGeography’s CommsUpdate: A really good newsletter that I truly look forward to every day

Wednesday, November 17, 20049:50 AM PST

There is no dearth of newsletters out there, but this one (TeleGeography’s CommsUpdate) is particularly interesting as it’s a daily newsletter which breaks down the news by geography in one simple newsletter. As the name suggests, the news is primarily focussed on Communications Industry.

If you’re interested, feel free to sign up or subscribe to their RSS feed using your favorite aggregator.
