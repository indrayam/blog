Launching ‘Induspolis.com’ linkblog

Monday, May 17, 20041:42 AM PDT

They say 2003 was a golden year for India in many respects. I tend to agree. But, while it might be comforting to know that things were great in 2003, I think 2004 and the next few years will be even more crucial in the history of this young nation. Needless to say, I am not the only one who feels this way. Countless Indian citizens and well-wishers share similar sentiments and are talking about it. Some are even using their blogs or online journals to share and/or vent.
