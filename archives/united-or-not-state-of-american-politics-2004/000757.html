Something is really wrong with the moderate Republicans..:-)

Saturday, October 30, 20045:11 PM PDT

First, it was Rudy Giuliani who on NBC’s “Today” show accused the troops for the “explosives being stolen” failure. Well, today it was Senator McCain’s turn to puke:

Wrapping up a campaign shadowed by war and terrorism, President Bush (\ and Democratic Sen. John Kerry unabashedly sought political advantage Saturday from Osama bin Laden’s re-emergence.

“It’s very helpful to the president,” contended Bush ally Sen. John McCain (news, bio, voting record), R-Ariz…

Somehow I did not expect Senator McCain to fall for this. He, of all people, is the most qualified to understand that not going after Osama Bin Laden in Tora Bora was indeed a bad idea. Oh well, I guess at the end of the day, Sen. McCain, like others is a politician by profession who will spin and say anything that helps his or his Party’s cause, irrespective of whether it’s right or wrong.

I guess I was expecting a bit too much from him.

- Anand
