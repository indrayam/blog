Wednesday, September 22, 200412:20 PM PDT

Do they have any substance to offer in the Iraq debate, any past record to brag about, any future plan that they’re particularly proud of.

Guess not!

The mantra is simple:

Kerry is a flip flopper. He voted for ‘blah’ and then voted for ‘halb’

Kerry is a flip flopper. He said ‘blah’ and then said ‘halb’

Kerry is a flip flopper. He hinted ‘blah’ and then said ‘halb’

Kerry is a flip flopper. Period.

…

I wish life was as boolean as some of these ads make it sound. Unfortunately, it is not. Anyone who thinks otherwise is living a lie.

Senator Kerry’s response to the Ad above: Juvenile
